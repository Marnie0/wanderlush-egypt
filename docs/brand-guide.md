# Wanderlush Egypt — brand and message guide

## Purpose

Most people arrive in Egypt with a list of monuments and leave with photographs
of a queue. Wanderlush Egypt exists so travellers design a journey around what
they actually want, understand what it costs before they commit, and arrive
knowing why each day is arranged the way it is.

**Tagline:** Experience Egypt beyond the ordinary
**Arabic tagline:** عِش مصر خارج المألوف

## Audience

| Segment | What they need from us |
| --- | --- |
| International first-timers | Distances, timings, costs and the confidence that a plan holds together |
| Egyptian residents | Weekend escapes, coastal and desert trips, prices in Egyptian pounds |
| Couples and honeymooners | Evenings, privacy, and a small number of very good moments |
| Families | Pacing, minimum ages, and an afternoon that is not another temple |
| Adventure travellers | Diving, camping, dunes, and honest difficulty ratings |

## Value proposition

A journey you design yourself, priced honestly, arranged by people who live here.

## Personality and tone

Confident, warm and specific. We sound like a well-travelled friend who happens
to live in Cairo, not like a brochure.

**We write:**

- Outcome first. "Arrive at opening and you get the plateau to yourself."
- Specific over decorative. Name the tomb, the hour, the temperature.
- Honest about friction. The balloon gets cancelled. January nights are cold.
- Short sentences. One idea each.

**We never write:**

- "Unforgettable", "hidden gem", "bucket list", "magical", "must-see".
- Superlatives we cannot support.
- Urgency or scarcity pressure of any kind.

## Arabic voice

Natural Modern Standard Arabic, not translated English. Sentences are built
for Arabic rhythm rather than mirrored from the English clause order. Numerals
stay Latin (١ → 1) because that matches Egyptian commercial usage and keeps
prices scannable.

## Messaging pillars

1. **Design your own journey.** Not a package. You choose and reorder.
2. **Know the price as you build.** The estimate moves with every change.
3. **Local knowledge, written down.** Every guide says when to arrive and why.
4. **Both languages, properly.** Arabic is a first-class experience, not a toggle.
5. **No account, no payment.** Explore and plan freely; a request is a request.

## Naming

- Company: Wanderlush Egypt / واندرلاش مصر
- The planner is "the trip builder", never "the wizard" or "the configurator".
- A saved plan is "your trip". A curated route we wrote is "a journey".
